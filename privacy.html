Privacy Policy

DikoWebAI / Fort McMurray Web & AI Services · Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information our automated Messenger assistant collects when you message our Facebook Page, how it is used, and how you can ask for it to be deleted.

Who we are

DikoWebAI is a local, owner-operated business run by Rod in Fort McMurray, Alberta. Our Facebook Page is Fort McMurray Web & AI Services (@FortMcMurrayWebAI), and our website is dikowebai.ca.

The Messenger assistant

When you send a message to our Facebook Page, you are talking with our automated frontdesk assistant. The assistant always identifies itself as automated and never pretends to be human. Rod, the owner, reads every message personally and replies within the day.

The assistant answers common questions — about our services, the free sample, and how to get started — strictly from our approved knowledge base. If a question is outside that knowledge base, or asks for a price beyond the pilot offer, the assistant passes it to Rod, who confirms it personally.

The assistant never asks for a password, card number, or any other sensitive information. Please never send passwords, card numbers, or other sensitive information to us through Messenger.

What information is received and how it is used

When you message our Page, Meta sends your message to us through its webhook so the assistant can reply. Your message is processed as follows:

  • To generate a reply: your message is sent to the Grok API (xAI), which generates the assistant’s reply strictly from our approved knowledge base.
  • To deliver the reply: the reply is sent back to you through Meta’s Messenger Send API.

Your message text is used only to generate and deliver the reply. Your message text is never logged and never stored.

What is stored

We keep a simple lead log so Rod can follow up on inquiries. Each lead record keeps only:

  • your Page-scoped ID (the identifier Meta assigns to you for our Page),
  • the category of what you asked about (for example, a price inquiry or a free sample request),
  • the date, and
  • a name or business name — only if you volunteer it in your message.

At most one lead entry is kept per visitor per day. If a question is passed to Rod, we also keep a short escalation record: your Page-scoped ID, the date and time, and the reason it was escalated.

The assistant’s short conversation memory (the last few turns) is kept only in memory while the conversation is active. It is never saved to disk.

All stored records live on the business’s own server.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your information is shared only with the two services needed to run the assistant: xAI (Grok), to generate replies, and Meta, to deliver them through Messenger. It is not shared with any other third party.

Your choices and deletion

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you at any time. Message our Facebook Page, or contact the owner directly:

  • Messenger: m.me/FortMcMurrayWebAI
  • Website: dikowebai.ca
  • Phone: (780) 881-5336

We will delete your lead and escalation records on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
